# DeconArt — next conversion job

The current website sells the approved microcement scope (wall, floor, wet
areas, selective ceilings, stairs, skirting, and iron doors) and keeps the
table collection as a separate prelaunch interest path without inventing
product details.

## Confirm before launching table sales

- Choose the first product: dining table or coffee table.
- Confirm exact width, length, finished height, top thickness, and one leg design.
- Set the retail price and state exactly what it includes.
- Define lead time, deposit, balance-payment timing, delivery area, delivery fee, and installation/assembly responsibility.
- Define warranty, returns/cancellations, care instructions, stain/heat/water limitations, and repair policy.
- Confirm which 2–3 finishes are actually available for the first release.
- Produce and photograph at least one real finished table before presenting it as a purchasable product.

## Conversion assets worth collecting

- 6–10 real completed-project photos with permission to publish.
- 2–3 short customer testimonials with a first name or company name and permission to quote.
- A clear answer on whether Ioseliani St. 107 is a showroom, workshop, office, or address by appointment only.
- Working hours and the normal response-time expectation.
- A verified Instagram URL, if one exists.
- A short wet-area and cleaning guide approved by the installer.

## Next website iteration

Once the items above are confirmed, add one focused product page for the first table, real photography, delivery/payment terms, a table-specific FAQ, and a deposit or order flow. Do not build a full catalog until the first product converts.
